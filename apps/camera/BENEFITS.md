# GoreeCloud Camera — Planned Benefits

> Repository document version: **0.1.0**  
> Product internal version: **0.1.0**  
> Release lifecycle: **Concept**

These are **intended product benefits**, not claims about an implemented application.

## User ownership

- Ordinary photos and videos remain user-owned files.
- Core capture is intended to work without a server or account.
- Media should remain portable if GoreeCloud Camera is unavailable.

## Privacy

- Local-first processing.
- Ephemeral viewfinder analysis by default.
- Explicit metadata policies.
- Private Capture with stronger restrictions.
- No silent upload of camera frames by Lens packages.

## Capability-aware experience

- Controls are exposed only when hardware actually supports them.
- Device-specific qualification can correct inaccurate vendor/API capability claims.
- Advanced features can degrade gracefully instead of failing unpredictably.

## Professional and creative depth

One application is intended to cover everyday capture, professional controls, filmmaking, scanning, creator workflows, multi-camera capture, effects, and privacy-sensitive capture without fragmenting the experience across unrelated apps.

## Resilience

Capture finalization, recording recovery, storage protection, thermal behavior, and interruption handling are treated as first-class product capabilities.

## GoreeCloud integration without lock-in

Camera is intended to integrate with Gallery, Photos, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, Glaze UI, Mesh, Identity, and other applicable GoreeCloud systems while keeping basic capture independently useful.
