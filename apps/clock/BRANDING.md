# GoreeCloud Clock branding

No canonical GoreeCloud Clock product icon from `GoreeCloud/branding-assets` was verified during this initial application-foundation tranche.

The vector at `src/main/res/drawable/ic_clock_app.xml` is therefore a simple repository-local Development identity used to keep the app independently installable and testable. It must not be represented as the final canonical GoreeCloud Clock brand asset.

Before Release Candidate qualification, verify or establish the canonical Clock product identity in the authoritative branding repository and replace this Development asset with exact provenance recorded here.
